How it works

Three steps. Two wallet prompts. Real numbers.

  1. 01

    Point it at a coin

    Paste any Solana token. Cascade reads its token accounts through Helius (up to 5,000), drops pools, curves, program-owned accounts, burn addresses and dust, merges wallets, and ranks the rest.

    25 · 50 · 100 · 200 · 500recipients you choose

  2. 02

    Launch on pump.fun

    Your coin is created with you as creator, your dev buy is quoted from pump's live curve, and every token of it is split pro rata by balance or equally. The table is exact down to the last base unit.

    0.01 SOLCascade fee, once, in the create tx

  3. 03

    It rains

    You sign every airdrop batch in one prompt. Each recipient needs a Token-2022 account: that rent is the real cost of being born with holders, and it is shown before you sign.

    0.00207 SOLaccount rent per recipient

NRecipient rent+ feeBatches

Rent = N × rent-exempt minimum for a 170-byte Token-2022 account, read from chain. Network fees and your dev buy come on top; the desk shows the full total.
